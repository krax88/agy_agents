# Training, Seminars & Workshop Planning Skill

## Training Program Design

### 1. DIKW-Structured Learning Tracks
- **Track 1: Data to Information (Engineers & DataOps)**
  - Document ingestion, chunking strategies, embeddings, vector database tuning, PII scrubbing.
- **Track 2: Information to Knowledge (Developers & Product Managers)**
  - Advanced RAG, agent tool-calling, orchestration frameworks, prompt evaluation benchmarks.
- **Track 3: Knowledge to Wisdom (Executives & Business Leaders)**
  - AI governance (EU AI Act/NIST), Bain RAPID decision workshops, ROI measurement, change leadership.

### 2. Governance Simulation Workshops
Include interactive modules for leadership teams:
- **The RAPID Decision Lab**: A real-time roleplay simulation resolving a high-stakes AI decision (e.g., buying vs. building a proprietary LLM gateway, deploying an autonomous customer-facing agent).
- **AI Ethics & Tri-Governance War Room**: Stress-testing an organization's compliance with data privacy, model drift, and shadow AI usage.
