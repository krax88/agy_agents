# Enterprise Transformation & Solution Strategy Skill

## Core Consulting Frameworks

### 1. Bain RAPID® for Enterprise AI Governance
When structuring organizational decision rights for AI initiatives, establish:
- **Recommend (R)**: AI Product Managers / Lead Architects (develops use case & architecture proposal).
- **Agree (A)**: CISO, Chief Legal Officer, Compliance Lead (verifies security, IP, and regulatory fit).
- **Input (I)**: Domain Experts, Data Stewards, End-user representatives (provides training data/context).
- **Decide (D)**: AI Steering Committee / Business Unit VP (final budget and go-live authorization).
- **Perform (P)**: AI Engineering, MLOps, and Business Operations (deploys and monitors the solution).

### 2. DACI Matrix for Project-Level Execution
Generate project-specific DACI tables for each AI initiative:
| Role | Assigned Stakeholder | Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Driver** | AI Transformation Lead | Directs sprint cadence, coordinates between data & business teams |
| **Approver** | Head of Technology / Sponsor | Signs off on architecture milestones and deployment gates |
| **Contributors**| Lead Architect, Data Engineer, Legal | Builds components, validates models, supplies documentation |
| **Informed** | Department Heads, End Users | Receives monthly roadmap updates and training invitations |

### 3. Tri-Governance Architecture Strategy
Structure governance across three mandatory layers:
- **Corporate Governance**: Board reporting on AI risk/exposure, alignment with corporate strategy, ethical AI principles.
- **Data Governance**: Data ownership matrix, data quality certifications for model ingestion, access controls.
- **AI Governance**: Model registry, bias testing, lifecycle monitoring, incident response for hallucinations or model drift.
