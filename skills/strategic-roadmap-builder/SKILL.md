# Strategic Roadmap Builder Skill

## When to use this skill
Use this skill when the user asks to:
- Build a 1-to-3 year enterprise AI strategic roadmap.
- Formulate milestone schedules, capability maturity progressions, and investment phases.
- Map strategic workstreams (Governance, Data/Platform, AI/Apps, People/Enablement, Value Realization).
- Structure roadmaps according to the DIKW pyramid and capability maturity levels.

## Roadmap Architecture Standards

### 1. The 3-Horizon Transformation Model
Always structure roadmaps across three clear horizons:

```text
=============================================================================================
HORIZON 1: Foundation & Quick Wins (M0 – M6)       --> Focus: Data to Information (DIKW: D->I)
HORIZON 2: Scaling & Platformization (M6 – M18)    --> Focus: Information to Knowledge (DIKW: I->K)
HORIZON 3: Differentiation & Autonomy (M18 – M36)  --> Focus: Knowledge to Wisdom (DIKW: K->W)
=============================================================================================
```

### 2. Multi-Workstream Matrix
Every roadmap deliverable must track five parallel workstreams:
1. **Governance & Risk**: Tri-governance baseline, EU AI Act/ISO 42001 compliance, DACI assignments.
2. **Data & Infrastructure**: Ingestion pipelines, vector storage, data lakehouse integration, MDM.
3. **AI & Solutions**: Lighthouse use cases, internal assistants, multi-agent automated workflows.
4. **People & Operating Model**: AI Center of Excellence (CoE), upskilling tracks, change management.
5. **Value Realization & KPIs**: Cost savings, cycle time reductions, revenue growth benchmarks.

### 3. Deliverable Output Template
When producing a Strategic Roadmap, provide:
1. **Executive Strategic Intent**: Vision, core thesis, and primary investment objectives.
2. **DIKW Alignment View**: Table mapping raw data sources to high-value strategic wisdom outcomes.
3. **Horizon Gantt / Milestone Table**:
   | Workstream | Horizon 1 (M0-M6) | Horizon 2 (M6-M18) | Horizon 3 (M18-M36) |
   | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
   | **Governance** | Tri-Governance Charter, Bain RAPID for AI | Model Risk Framework, ISO 42001 | Autonomous Auditing & Continuous Compliance |
   | **Data Engine** | Data cataloging, embedding pipelines | Knowledge graph integration | Real-time agentic context mesh |
   | **AI Solutions** | Lighthouse RAG & Internal Assistant | Multi-Agent cross-functional workflows| Custom fine-tuned domain models |
   | **Talent & Org** | Executive briefing & DACI operational model | Enterprise Upskilling Academy | Embedded AI product squads |
4. **Critical Path & Risk Register**: Top 3 critical dependencies and mitigation strategies.
